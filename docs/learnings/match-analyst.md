- [work] 2026-10-22 Possession-first generated fixtures can produce hundreds of routine passes and many pressure/tackle actions; raw positive action weights saturate the player activity rating. Guard rating distributions across multiple generator seeds and a sparse staged fixture, and report both before/after. (match-analyst)
